# FAQ

## Why does my button seem to have no hover effect?

The hover colors may be too close to the resting colors. Check the scheme in **Theme settings > Colors**. See [Colors and buttons](/colors-and-buttons).

## Can I turn off the button animation?

Yes. Clear **Theme settings > Buttons > Hover fill animation**. Button colors then fade instead.

## Why are collection filters missing?

Filters are managed in the Shopify **Search & Discovery** app. Check that filters are set up there and that **Enable filters** is on in the **Collection** section.

## Why does a product card not add to cart directly?

Products with options link to their product page, so shoppers can choose a variant first.

## Why don't sections fade in while I edit?

**Reveal content on scroll** is turned off inside the theme editor. Preview your store to see it.

## Which languages does the theme include?

English and Simplified Chinese. Text direction follows the store language, including right-to-left languages.
